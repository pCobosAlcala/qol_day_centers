Continuous rows show means and binary rows show percentages; Difference is the first group minus the second (percentage points for binary rows). p values come from Welch t-tests for continuous variables and chi-squared tests with continuity correction for binary ones. n is the number of people with data on each variable, first group / second group. One invited person has no Lawton score, Katz score or caregiver record; every other value is observed. Age is measured at the baseline interview, from birth dates. Education is coded 1 to 10 (1 none, 3 primary, 4 lower secondary, 9 university degree, 10 postgraduate). Cared for by a son or daughter refers to the main caregiver, including caregivers recorded as "Hija/o" (son or daughter). WHOQOL-BREF is the mean of the standard 26 items; it and the environmental domain are on the WHO 0 to 100 scale (25 times the item mean minus 1).
