Attrition by take up and by arm

Sample: all 120 people interviewed at baseline. Not surveyed means no interview at that wave, which is the same as being outside that wave's ANCOVA sample. Attended the centre means more than five days in the centre's attendance register over its whole period (18 January 2023 to 16 February 2024), so that every person has a group, whether surveyed or not. The main results instead count days up to each wave's survey, which exists only for people surveyed at that wave. Of the 33 people who attended over the whole register, 28 were attending by their midline survey (three began attending after that survey and two were not surveyed at midline) and 24 by their endline survey (the other nine were not surveyed at endline). Nobody who was not invited attended. Panel B tests whether the share not surveyed is the same in the groups named in each row (Fisher exact test on the groups by surveyed table). The take up groups are defined after randomisation: to count as attending, a person had to stay alive and available, so differences between take up groups describe who stayed rather than test for attrition bias. Only the comparison by arm (invited versus never invited) tests differential attrition between the randomised groups.
