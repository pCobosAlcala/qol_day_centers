First stage: what the invitation delivered

Each exposure is regressed on the randomised invitation and baseline WHOQOL-BREF. Robust (HC2) standard errors in parentheses; F is the robust F on the invitation. Attending means more than five days at the centre up to that wave's survey; weeks attended are those days divided by five; days per week are days attended per seven calendar days since the first visit. Nobody who was not invited attended the centre, so non-compliance is one-sided and all three exposures are zero in that group. The 24 people attending by endline had attended 20 weeks on average, at 2.5 days per week. *** p < 0.01, ** p < 0.05, * p < 0.1.
