Effects on the eight items of the environmental domain (WHOQOL-BREF)

Item level estimates are exploratory: each item is a single answer on a five point scale, and eight items are tested at each wave. Each cell reports the estimate in points of the WHO 0 to 100 scale (25 times the answer minus 1, so an item takes the values 0, 25, 50, 75 or 100), the robust (HC2) standard error in parentheses, the unadjusted p value and the Holm adjusted p value. The Holm adjustment is over the eight items, separately for each wave and for ITT and IV. Each item is estimated as in the main results (Table 3): ANCOVA of the item at each wave on its own baseline value and the randomised invitation, separately for each wave. ITT is the effect of the invitation. IV is the effect of attending, instrumented by the invitation with the baseline value in both stages; attending means more than five days at the centre by that wave's survey (months are counted from entry for those attending). Control baseline is the item's mean at baseline among those never invited, in that wave's sample. The last row is the domain estimate of Table 3 (the mean of the eight items), which is not part of the adjustment. First-stage F is the robust F on the excluded instrument, shown as the range across the nine regressions. Items are numbered as in the WHO form and in section 16 of the study questionnaire. Stars use the unadjusted p value: *** p < 0.01, ** p < 0.05, * p < 0.1.
