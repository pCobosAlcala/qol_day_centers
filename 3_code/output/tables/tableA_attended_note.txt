Who took up the offer: invited people who attended and who did not

Sample: people invited in one of the eight randomisation rounds. Attended means more than five days at the centre (more than one week). Panel A counts days over the centre's whole attendance register (18 January 2023 to 16 February 2024), so it classifies every invited person, including those not surveyed after baseline. Panels B and C count days up to that wave's survey, the attendance measure of the main results, among the invited people of each ANCOVA sample. Of the 33 people who attended over the whole register, 28 were attending by their midline survey (three began attending after that survey and two were not surveyed at midline) and 24 by their endline survey (the other nine were not surveyed at endline). Nobody who was not invited attended. The comparison describes who took up the offer (compliers) against invited people who did not (never takers). Continuous rows show means and binary rows show percentages; Difference is the first group minus the second (percentage points for binary rows). p values come from Welch t-tests for continuous variables and chi-squared tests with continuity correction for binary ones. n is the number of people with data on each variable, first group / second group. One invited person has no Lawton score, Katz score or caregiver record; every other value is observed. Fisher exact tests on the binary rows give the same conclusions at the 5 and 10 per cent levels. Age is measured at the baseline interview, from birth dates. Education is coded 1 to 10 (1 none, 3 primary, 4 lower secondary, 9 university degree, 10 postgraduate). Cared for by a son or daughter refers to the main caregiver, including caregivers recorded as "Hija/o" (son or daughter). WHOQOL-BREF is the mean of the standard 26 items; it and the environmental domain are on the WHO 0 to 100 scale (25 times the item mean minus 1). With ten comparisons per panel the Bonferroni threshold for a 5 per cent test is 0.005; no difference passes it under either test (the smallest p value is 0.216).
