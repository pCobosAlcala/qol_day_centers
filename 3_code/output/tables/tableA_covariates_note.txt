Effect of the day centre with added baseline covariates

Each cell reports the estimate in points of the WHO 0 to 100 scale, the robust (HC2) standard error in parentheses and the p value. ANCOVA run separately for each wave: the outcome at that wave is regressed on its own baseline value and either the randomised invitation (ITT, the effect of the invitation) or attendance instrumented by the invitation (IV, the effect of attending; attending means more than five days at the centre by that wave's survey), plus the covariates of the column. (a) Main specification, no covariates. (b) Adds the baseline Katz (basic activities of daily living, 0 to 6) and Lawton (instrumental activities, 0 to 8) scores. (c) Adds age at baseline, sex, indigenous, household size, education (ten levels, entered as a number) and whether the main caregiver is a son or daughter. (d) Adds both sets. (e) Lin (2013): the invitation is fully interacted with the baseline outcome, Katz and Lawton, each centred at its sample mean; the IV version keeps these interactions and instruments attendance with the invitation alone. Sample: everyone observed at baseline and at that wave; people missing a covariate drop out of that column (row Individuals). First-stage F is the robust F on the invitation, shown as the range over the two IV outcomes. IV estimates for the other domains are available on request. Months are counted from entry to the centre for those attending. WHOQOL-BREF is the mean of the standard 26 items; all scores are on the WHO 0 to 100 scale (25 times the item mean minus 1). *** p < 0.01, ** p < 0.05, * p < 0.1.
