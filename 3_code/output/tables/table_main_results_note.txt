Effect of the day centre on quality of life (WHOQOL-BREF)

Each cell reports the estimate in points of the WHO 0 to 100 scale, the robust (HC2) standard error in parentheses and the 95 per cent confidence interval in brackets. ANCOVA: the outcome at each wave is regressed on its own baseline value and the randomised invitation, separately for each wave. ITT is the effect of the invitation (the offer of a place). IV is the effect of attending, with attendance instrumented by the invitation and the baseline value in both stages; attending means more than five days at the centre by that wave's survey. Each wave uses everyone observed at baseline and at that wave. Months are counted from entry to the centre for those attending; those not attending were surveyed about six months and a year after baseline. First-stage F is the robust F on the excluded instrument, shown as the range across the six outcomes. WHOQOL-BREF is the mean of the standard 26 items; all scores are on the WHO 0 to 100 scale (25 times the item mean minus 1). *** p < 0.01, ** p < 0.05, * p < 0.1.
