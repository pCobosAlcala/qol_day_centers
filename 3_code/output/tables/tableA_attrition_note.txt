Attrition: baseline characteristics of people retained and not retained

Sample: all 120 people interviewed at baseline. Retained means observed at baseline and midline (Panel A, the midline ANCOVA sample), at baseline and endline (Panel B, the endline ANCOVA sample) or at all three waves (Panel C). Three of the 38 not retained in Panel C were observed at endline and are in the endline sample; they missed only the midline interview. Attrition by arm: 12 of 91 invited (13.2 per cent) and 6 of 29 never invited (20.7 per cent; Fisher exact p = 0.373) were not surveyed at midline, and 29 of 91 invited (31.9 per cent) and 6 of 29 never invited (20.7 per cent; Fisher exact p = 0.349) at endline. Rates by take up are in Table A.7. Continuous rows show means and binary rows show percentages; Difference is the first group minus the second (percentage points for binary rows). p values come from Welch t-tests for continuous variables and chi-squared tests with continuity correction for binary ones. n is the number of people with data on each variable, first group / second group. One invited person has no Lawton score, Katz score or caregiver record; every other value is observed. Fisher exact tests on the binary rows give the same conclusions at the 5 and 10 per cent levels. Age is measured at the baseline interview, from birth dates. Education is coded 1 to 10 (1 none, 3 primary, 4 lower secondary, 9 university degree, 10 postgraduate). Cared for by a son or daughter refers to the main caregiver, including caregivers recorded as "Hija/o" (son or daughter). WHOQOL-BREF is the mean of the standard 26 items; it and the environmental domain are on the WHO 0 to 100 scale (25 times the item mean minus 1). With ten comparisons per panel the Bonferroni threshold for a 5 per cent test is 0.005; one passes it: age at baseline, Panel A (p = 0.003).
