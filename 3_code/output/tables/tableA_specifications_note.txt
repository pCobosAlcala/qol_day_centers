Effect of the day centre under alternative specifications

Each cell reports the estimate in points of the WHO 0 to 100 scale, the standard error in parentheses and the p value. Attending (IV) is the effect of attending, with attendance (more than five days at the centre by that wave's survey) instrumented by the randomised invitation; Invited (ITT) is the effect of the invitation. (a) Main specification: the outcome at each wave regressed on its own baseline value and the invitation, separately for each wave; robust (HC2) errors; everyone observed at baseline and at that wave. (b) Both follow-up waves stacked, one row per person and wave, with a wave dummy, the baseline value and the invitation: one average effect over the two waves. (c) Difference in differences on all three waves: invitation times midline and invitation times endline, with wave dummies and individual fixed effects (for IV, attendance at each wave instrumented by invitation times that wave). (b) and (c) use the 105 people observed at baseline and at least once more; people seen only at baseline drop out; errors clustered by person (CR2), p values from a t distribution with 104 degrees of freedom. (d) As (c) with a random intercept per person instead of fixed effects; all 120 people with a baseline; model based errors, Satterthwaite p values; no IV version. (e) Not randomised: the ANCOVA of (a) with actual attendance in place of the invitation, by OLS with HC2 errors. It compares the people who chose to attend (24 at endline) with everyone else, invited or not (61), so it is subject to self selection and is not an estimate of the effect of the centre. WHOQOL-BREF is the mean of the standard 26 items; all scores are on the WHO 0 to 100 scale (25 times the item mean minus 1). *** p < 0.01, ** p < 0.05, * p < 0.1.
